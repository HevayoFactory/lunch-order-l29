# Order list

## Purpose

The office manager sees who ordered what for the day.

Needs: F2.

## User Stories

- F4.1 As an office manager, I see today's orders listed per person, each with the items they ordered.
- F4.2 As an office manager, I see the list while ordering is open and after it is closed.
- F4.3 As an office manager, I see the list reflect staff changes and cancellations as they happen.
- F4.4 As an office manager, I see the number of people who have ordered.
- F4.5 As an office manager, I see a clear empty state when nobody has ordered yet.

## Decisions

- The order list is per person; there is no per-item totals view.
- The list is on-screen only.
- Only the office manager can see everyone's orders; staff see only their own.

## Out of Scope

- Per-item totals view.
- Exporting or printing the list.
